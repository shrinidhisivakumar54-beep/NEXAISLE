## Required delivery for each reference

- A licensed, exact-reference GLB/GLTF with physically correct case silhouette, crown, bezel, crystal, dial, hands and bracelet. Include material textures and a redistribution/web-use licence.
- Separate, correctly positioned nodes named `crystal`, `bezel`, `dial`, `hour`, `minute`, `second`, `movement`, `case`, `crown`, `back`, and `strap`; include `date` and `pushers` where appropriate.
- Verified movement assembly supplied or reviewed by a qualified modeller/watchmaker. Do not relabel a generic procedural wheel train as Powermatic 80 or G10.212.
- Original pivot locations and assembled transforms, with no negative scale. Use metres or provide the unit scale. Include reference photographs, attribution and author/licence files.
- A real model is needed to fulfil continuous product rotation, true product 360° inspection, and authentic component explosion. The current photographic fallback deliberately does not simulate those capabilities with flat image planes.
