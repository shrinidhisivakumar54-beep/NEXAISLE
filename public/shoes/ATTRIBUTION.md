## Anatomy limitations
The Shopify runner and GSO scan are exterior meshes. No internal layers are invented. Supply component-separated GLB files with upper, laces, insole, midsole and outsole nodes to enable disassembly for those models. Size conversions are generic adult estimates, not manufacturer size charts. Foot scanning is a local photo/camera preview demo, with no measurement output.
